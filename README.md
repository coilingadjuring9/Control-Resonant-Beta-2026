## 🕹️ About

CONTROL Resonant is a third-person action-adventure RPG from Remedy Entertainment, set in a paranaturally warped Manhattan. Play as Dylan Faden, wield evolving abilities and the shapeshifting Aberrant weapon while searching for his missing sister, Jesse. Join our beta testing program, help shape the pre-release experience, and receive a free game key for participating.

CONTROL Resonant is for players who enjoy cinematic supernatural adventures, reality-bending worlds, deep action-RPG progression, and story-driven exploration.

> ⚡ **This is an exclusive beta test opportunity!** Join BETABRIDGE to get early access, help shape the game's development, and earn a free Steam key upon release.

## ✨ Features

- 🌆 **Warped Manhattan** — Explore a reality-bending version of New York transformed by paranatural forces.
- ⚔️ **Evolving Combat** — Combine elemental abilities, environmental attacks, and close-range combat.
- 🌀 **The Aberrant** — Transform Dylan’s shapeshifting melee weapon to match every threat.
- 🧩 **Buildcrafting** — Develop Dylan through flexible abilities, upgrades, and tactical playstyles.
- 👁️ **Paranatural Mystery** — Uncover a supernatural crisis through story missions, secrets, and puzzles.
- 🎮 **Beta Community** — Test pre-release content, share feedback, and help refine the final experience.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: What is BETABRIDGE?**
**A:** BETABRIDGE is a beta testing platform that connects gamers with game developers. By joining our beta program, you get early access to upcoming games and help shape their development through honest feedback.

**Q: How does beta testing work?**
**A:** Simply sign in with your Steam account, browse available beta programs, request access to games you're interested in, and start testing. After you complete the beta, you'll receive a free Steam key for the full game upon its official release.

**Q: Do I need to pay to join?**
**A:** No, joining a beta test is completely free. All we ask for is your time and genuine feedback.

**Q: Is my Steam account safe?**
**A:** Yes. We use Steam's official sign-in, so we never see or store your password. Your account stays fully under your control.

**Q: When will I receive the Steam key?**
**A:** Keys are sent via email once the game you tested officially releases on Steam. We'll notify you at the email address associated with your Steam account.

**Q: Will I get access right away?**
**A:** After you sign in with Steam, your tester profile needs to be approved. Approvals go out as spots free up — roughly once a week. Once approved, beta tests become visible and requestable.

**Q: What is the reward for testing?**
**A:** Your reward is the game itself — a free Steam key as soon as it officially launches. No points, no gimmicks, just the real thing.
